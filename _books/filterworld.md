Filterworld is an interesting book. It makes you think about how the advances in technology are changing the way we live and our freedom to chose what we really like, and most critically, our perception of ourselfs and who we really are.

It argues about how the algorithms - mostly the feeds -, are making culture homogeneous. Suddenly, everyone is seeing the same shows, listening to the same music, reading the same books, wearing the same clothes, and so on and so forth.
