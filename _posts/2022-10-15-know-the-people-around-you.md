I do this super simple exercise from time to time.

I open up a notepad and write down for each of my colleagues the following:

Person A -> **Does** XYZ and **Likes** ABC

Here's a real example from one my exercises, which coincidentally goes from A to Z (names omitted of course):

As you can see, I had some gaps to fulfill. I knew an interesting fact of only 14 people out of 26.

I used this quick exercise to identify which person I should talk more and explore a more personal connection and which ones I already had that established.

When you are working in positions where you have to lead/mentor/coach people or where you have to manage the work people do, it is extremely important to show the minimum level of interest on them, otherwise your exchanges will become super artificial.

I experienced this many times in my career with people that I had some difficulties to build a more personal and open relationship. Usually 1:1s were not the most productive and valuable ones and the exchanges were more energy draining than energizing.

> **A good sign you had a good exchange with your colleague is if you left the conversation in a light mood and energized.**

Pay attention to that and you can refine your feeling in regard to how valuable the moments you are investing to know your colleagues better has been. Also, it is a good exercise of self-awarenes and a valuable source of feedback for you to get better at communication and to identify gaps in your soft skills.

I hope that helps someone to fill the gaps and to know better the people around you.
